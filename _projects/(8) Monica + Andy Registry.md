## The problem

A registry that only lists your own catalogue isn't much use to an expecting parent who wants one list for everything. A *universal* registry has to accept items from anywhere on the web, keep them current, and still feel like one coherent product.

## What I built

**Universal registry** — aggregate items from any store into a single list, with a guided checklist for first-time parents who don't yet know what they need.

**Gift funds** — contribute money toward a larger item instead of buying it outright, which needs careful handling of partial contributions and refunds.

**Shopify, extended** — custom plugins plus an extended inventory management module for tighter stock control than the platform offers out of the box.

**Payments** — a custom **Dwolla** integration for ACH transfers, built with the security posture that moving other people's money requires.

**API layer** — GraphQL with Graphene on Django, which cut over-fetching on registry views where a single page pulls from several resources.
