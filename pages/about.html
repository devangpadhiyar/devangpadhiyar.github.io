I'm a backend-heavy generalist who ended up building the thing that builds the thing. Nine years in, my work has moved from enterprise ERP through crypto and e-commerce into AI agents — and the throughline is systems that have to keep running when something fails halfway.

Right now that means two things. At Simplified I led the AI video team of up to five engineers — text-to-video, AI voices and cloning, captions, long-form repurposed into clips, 577K+ videos made — and now build its production LLM agents end to end. On the side I'm the founding engineer of Scenes AI, an agent-driven video studio backed by Simplified, where the hard part is keeping a character recognisably themselves across six episodes.

The rest of my time goes to agent infrastructure. I wrote Langrove, a self-hosted drop-in alternative to LangGraph Cloud, and I send fixes upstream to the agent frameworks I build on, like Mastra — mostly to the unglamorous half, where runs resume on a different worker than they started on and traces have to survive the hop.

What I keep running into

Nine years, five domains, and the same few problems wearing different clothes.

Two renderers always drift apart

A canvas preview and a frame-by-frame render disagree the moment an asset loads late. Most "rendering bugs" are readiness bugs in costume.

Simplified · video export

State that outlives the process

A run that can't resume on a worker that never saw it start isn't durable. Same problem as escrow that must survive a node restart.

Langrove · LocalCoinSwap

Someone else's client is the spec

Building a drop-in replacement, or a Shopify extension, means the existing API is fixed. You don't get to redesign it to suit your implementation.

Langrove · Monica + Andy

The demo is not the product

Cutting one good clip from an hour of footage is a demo. Doing it repeatably, at publishable quality, is months.

Simplified · repurpose

Text is never just text

Canvas has no wrapping, no kerning, no line box. Animate per character and you destroy the shaping — silently, and only for some languages.

Simplified · captions

Deterministic beats clever

Anything reading the wall clock, or an unseeded random, makes a bug unfalsifiable. Render as a pure function of frame and document.
